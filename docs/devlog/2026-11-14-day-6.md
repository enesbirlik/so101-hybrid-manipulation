# Day 6 — The shoulder zero was wrong, and a ruler showed it

**Date:** 2026-11-14 · **Roadmap step:** 4. C++ `ros2_control` interface + MoveIt 2 (model accuracy on the real arm) · **Status:** in progress

Every reachable screw was tightened in the morning. The goal for the day: the model should match the real arm. Two complaints:
1. When the hand touches the table, the model thinks it is inside the table.
2. `shoulder_pan` sometimes shakes while turning.

Both turned out to have simple causes. The first one was found by a method that had been written off in [day 4](2026-11-01-day-4.md).

## What I did

**Housekeeping** (commits on `feat/phase4-ros2-control`, not pushed):
- [`so101_teach_check.py`](../../so101_bringup/scripts/so101_teach_check.py) compares taught (torque off) poses with held and replayed (torque on) poses. `scan` in the tools now prints P/D/I and the dead zone of every servo.
- `so101.urdf.xacro` prints a warning when `table_z` is missing from `so101_kinematics.yaml`. A kinematic fit removes that key on purpose, and the table then silently moved to the bottom of the base.
- An offline consistency test, [`test_calibration_consistency.py`](../../so101_bringup/test/test_calibration_consistency.py), compares the calibration, the URDF, the MoveIt limits, the SRDF poses and `table_z`: 39 cases, 37 pass, 2 are strict xfail.
  - It found one real inconsistency, the `elbow_flex` upper limit: 91.7° calibrated (3145 ticks), 92.8° in MoveIt, 96.8° in the URDF. The hardware interface clamps commands to the calibrated range.
- `scan` showed all servos at P/D/I 32/32/0 in EEPROM, dead zones 1/1, 12.4–12.5 V. `ros2_control` writes P 16, D 32, I 0 to RAM at activation, one value for all joints.

**The `shoulder_pan` shake.** A sweep script moved only the pan (±30°, then ±20°, trapezoid profile) with the arm stretched out at the zero pose, and logged reference, feedback and load at 100 Hz.
- Following error grows with speed: 1.6° at 0.3 rad/s, 4.9° at 0.75 rad/s, 11.4° (peak 13.3°) at 1.5 rad/s. The servo lags the command by about 0.1 s.
- At medium and high speed there is no shake. At low speed there is a ~4 Hz oscillation: 0.6–0.7° peak-to-peak at 0.05 rad/s, with 13–29 stalls per move (more than 80 ms without one encoder tick), and 0.2–0.5° at 0.1–0.3 rad/s. Watching the arm gave the same answer: smooth when fast, visibly shaking when slow.
- After each stop 0.3–0.5° of error remains, and the load does not return to zero (±3 %).
- No bus errors, steady voltage, the loop ran at 100.0 Hz. So it is not communication or power.

**The table.**
- *First attempt, failed.* Lower the tool straight down in 1 mm steps and detect contact from the servo load.
  - The load changes by only 2–4 % at contact. Holding versus lowering already differs by 8 % (the friction changes side), which gave a false contact at step 1.
  - After the hand touched, it kept pushing and sliding forward.
  - With that method the model read −12, −17 and −19 mm at 220, 290 and 350 mm reach. That overstates the error.
  - Side finding: on the way back up the arm did not reach the commanded height. It was 12 mm short at 220 mm reach, 19 mm at 290 mm and 30 mm at 350 mm. `shoulder_lift` was 1.4°, 2.6° and 4.4° behind its goal, with load up to 21 %. The encoders see this, so the model is right, but the arm is not where it was sent.
- *What worked.* Stop at a height and read the gap under the lowest point of the hand with a ruler, torque on, hand in the air. This is the same method as the "gentler check" of day 4, but in more poses.
  - Eight poses, reach 168–347 mm, tool pitch −20° to −80°, one high pose. The model was 3 to 16 mm *below* the real hand, growing with reach: 3 mm at 168 mm, 9–10 mm at 215 mm, 14 mm at 287 mm, 16 mm at 347 mm.
  - So the model was wrong in free space, before any contact.
  - A fit over the first six poses, then a blind check on two more poses with a different tool pitch: the old model was 6.7 and 11.9 mm off there, the corrected one 3.5 and 0.2 mm.
  - `shoulder_lift` −5.65° with the table at the bottom of the base (−2.4 mm, where the base stands) takes the eight gaps from 10.3 mm rms to 2.4 mm rms (leave-one-out 2.8 mm). Whatever else was freed in the fit (table height, elbow, wrist), the shoulder correction stayed between −4.0° and −5.7°.
- *The number was seen before.* On 2026-11-01 the torque-off rests on the table asked for about −6° on `shoulder_lift` (day 4, "Finding the play"), and I put it down to play. That is the same −6°, in the same direction. The +10.5° of 2026-10-28, from the hand-eye table fit ([day 3](2026-10-28-day-3.md)), was about 5–6° too much. The `table_z` of −14.9 mm (12.5 mm below the base bottom) and probably part of the 11 mm `wrist_flex` shift were making up for that error.
- *Applied* (commit `01a8df6`): `shoulder_lift` zero 1929 → 1993 in [`so101_calibration.yaml`](../../so101_bringup/config/so101_calibration.yaml), and `table_z` −0.0149 → −0.0024 in [`so101_kinematics.yaml`](../../so101_description/config/so101_kinematics.yaml).

## Results

Check after the change, four new ruler poses (model vs ruler):

| Pose | Model | Ruler |
|---|---|---|
| 273 mm reach | 42.9 mm | 44 mm |
| 347 mm reach | 19.2 mm | 14.5 mm |
| 214 mm reach | 15.8 mm | 18 mm |
| 214 mm reach, just above the table | 0.7 mm | 4 mm |

- All twelve poses: 2.6 mm rms, worst 4.7 mm.
- At real contact the model now reads about −3 mm instead of −10 mm at that reach.
- What is left: the model is 2–4 mm low near the base and 3–5 mm high at full reach. `shoulder_lift` +1.5° together with `wrist_flex` −3.4° would bring it to about 1.7 mm rms. I did not apply it: twelve single ruler numbers cannot separate the wrist from the shoulder reliably.

Raw data stays outside the repo (`~/so101_data/ruler_gaps`, `table_contact`, `pan_test`). The two measuring scripts are not in the repo yet.

## What I learned

- **One angle error looks like a table-height error.** A wrong `shoulder_lift` zero rotates the whole arm about the shoulder. The error at the hand grows with reach, like the radius times the angle. A fit with a free table height pushes it into the table height, and the table sinks. That is what `table_z` = −14.9 mm was doing.
- **Measure in free space before touching anything.** Contact adds friction, play and sliding. A ruler under a hand that hangs in the air has none of these.
- **Earlier analysis can be wrong in both directions.** Earlier the same morning, an offline analysis of the 2026-11-01 data had concluded the opposite: free space is right to 1–2 mm, the error appears only through contact, a ~4.2° play. The ruler in free space settled it. Day 4's reading of the −6° as play was also wrong.
- **A held-out check is worth the extra poses.** The two blind poses with a different tool pitch told more than the six fitted ones.
- **The shake is stick-slip.** With P 16 the motor does not push through static friction until the error has built up (~0.6°), then it jumps. Every MoveIt move passes through this speed range while accelerating and braking, which is why the shake showed "sometimes". The sag on the way up (30 mm at full reach) has the same cause.

## Problems & fixes

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The model thinks the hand is inside the table at contact | `shoulder_lift` zero 5.65° off; `table_z` compensated | Zero 1929 → 1993, `table_z` −2.4 mm |
| Load-based contact detection gave −12 to −19 mm | Load changes 2–4 % at contact, friction changes side, the hand slides on | Not used. Ruler gaps in free space instead |
| The arm stops short on the way up (12–30 mm) | P 16 under load | **Open:** per-joint gains |
| `shoulder_pan` shakes when slow | Stick-slip at P 16 | **Open:** per-joint gains |
| `table_z` silently moved after a kinematic fit | The fit removes the key | The xacro prints a warning |
| Single temperature samples of 65–120 °C on servos at 32–36 °C, four times in about thirty moves | Not found yet. `so101_hardware` does not check the status byte of the reply, and the read timeout (20 ms) is longer than the control period (10 ms) | **Open** |

## Open

- **The hand-eye fit has to be redone.** The joint origins in `so101_kinematics.yaml` and the camera pose in `wrist_camera_hand_eye.yaml` were fitted with the old zero. Plan: shoulder zero free, ruler gaps as constraints, at least 3 board placements. It is needed before the camera is used for perception.
- **A hand resting on the table is still a start state in collision for MoveIt:** 3 mm inside is still inside. Lift off automatically before planning (`so101_lift.py` exists, the automatic version is not written).
- **Per-joint position gains** for the pan shake and the sag: in progress, not done.
- **Corrupt telemetry** in `so101_hardware`: check the status byte, and bring the read timeout below the control period.
- **`elbow_flex` range:** a `scan` showed the folded rest at 3148 ticks, while the servo's own EEPROM maximum is 3122. The range has to be measured again.
